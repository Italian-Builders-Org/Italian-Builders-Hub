# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Italian founders, developers, designers, operators, researchers, makers, and open-source contributors who want to discover one another, share work, and collaborate. Public visitors browse the community and its output; invited members maintain profiles and projects; admins operate access and curation workflows.

## Product Purpose

Italian Builders is the public home and member platform for a curated community of people who build in or are connected to Italy. Success means people can understand the mission, discover credible builders and projects, request access, and use the member platform without encountering a split or inconsistent experience.

## Brand Personality

Warm, crafted, and opinionated. The experience should feel distinctly Italian without leaning on generic flag imagery or startup-template conventions. It should communicate confidence, community, and the dignity of making real things.

## Anti-references

Generic SaaS landing pages, interchangeable card grids, decorative terminal cosplay, flag-heavy cultural stereotypes, emoji-led interface decoration, and parallel visual versions exposed as user-facing style choices.

## Design Principles

- Put real people and real work ahead of abstract claims.
- Treat public discovery and member workflows as one coherent product.
- Make visibility, access, and authorship boundaries explicit.
- Preserve a distinctive visual voice while keeping navigation and actions direct.
- Prefer complete, trustworthy content over decorative shortcuts.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic structure, keyboard-operable controls, clear focus states, meaningful alternative text, sufficient contrast, responsive layouts, and reduced-motion behavior where motion is present. Do not rely on color alone to convey state.
